Privacy policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

The short version

Whatever you paste into Diffhero is compared entirely in your own browser. It is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. Close the tab and it's gone.

What we collect

Your text and code: nothing. The diff runs client-side; your content never reaches a server we control. A share link, if you choose to create one, encodes your two inputs into the URL itself — it is not stored by us. Files you drag in are read locally in your browser and never uploaded.

Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymous usage (pages viewed, rough country, device type). It does not see the text you compare.

Advertising: We show ads via Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings. Visitors in the EEA/UK are shown a consent choice before any personalized ads load.

Cookies

Third-party cookies may be set by Google (for the services named above). The tool itself sets no tracking cookies of its own; any preferences (like light/dark theme or your split/unified choice) are stored locally in your browser and never transmitted.

Your rights

Because we don't hold your content or a profile of you, there's nothing on our side to access, export, or delete. For any third-party services above, refer to Google's privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@diffhero.app.
