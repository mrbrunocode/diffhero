Invisible Character Checker

Paste both versions and turn on “Show invisibles” in the toolbar: non-breaking spaces render as ⍽, zero-width characters as ∅, and tabs as →, so the character your eye can't see becomes the change you can. These sneak in constantly — Word and Google Docs convert straight quotes to curly ones, web pages copy out non-breaking spaces, and AI chat output often carries zero-width characters — and they make string comparisons, config parsers and spreadsheet lookups fail on text that looks identical. Use Find & replace below the tool to strip the culprit once you've spotted it.

Find & replace

Finding the invisible characters two texts don't share

Sometimes two strings look identical but aren't equal, and the culprit is a character you can't see: a non-breaking space instead of a normal space, a zero-width space, a smart quote pasted from a word processor, or a Windows \r\n line ending versus a Unix \n. This surfaces them.

What it catches

  • Whitespace impostors: non-breaking spaces (U+00A0), tabs, and trailing spaces.
  • Zero-width characters that occupy no visual space but break string comparisons and sometimes sneak in from copied web text.
  • Smart punctuation (curly quotes, en/em dashes) swapped for their ASCII equivalents.
  • Line-ending mismatches between two otherwise-identical files.

Turn on Show invisibles to render these explicitly. It's the tool for "these two are the same — so why does the comparison fail?" All local, nothing uploaded.

Common questions

What invisible characters does it reveal?

Non-breaking spaces (U+00A0, plus the narrow and figure variants), zero-width space/joiner/non-joiner (U+200B–U+200D), word joiner, the byte-order mark (U+FEFF), soft hyphens and tabs. Each renders as a distinct visible symbol with a tooltip naming it.

Where do these characters come from?

The usual suspects: copying from Word or Google Docs (curly quotes, non-breaking spaces), copying from web pages (non-breaking spaces in prices and names), and text produced by chat AI tools (zero-width characters). Keyboards can also type them — Option+Space on a Mac inserts a non-breaking space.

How do I remove them once found?

Open Find & replace under the tool, paste the offending character into Find (copy it straight from your text), leave Replace empty, and apply to both sides. For non-breaking spaces, replace with a normal space instead so words don't merge.
