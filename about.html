About Diffhero

Diffhero is a small, single-purpose tool: paste two versions of any text or code and instantly see what changed — every added, removed and altered line, with the exact changed words highlighted inline.

It's deliberately simple. There's no account to make, nothing to install, and no data to hand over — the whole comparison runs in your browser, so your text and code never touch a server. That also means it works offline once the page has loaded, and it's safe to paste things you couldn't upload to someone else's site.

Why another diff checker? Because the popular ones wall the good parts behind signups, daily limits or a paywall. Diffhero keeps the useful features — word-level highlighting, split and unified views, file drag-and-drop, shareable links and .diff export — genuinely free, with nothing uploaded.

It stays free by showing a single, unobtrusive ad, and nothing more. Found a bug or want a feature? Get in touch.
